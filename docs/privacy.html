Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

This policy explains what personal data Zain Siddiq, trading as Lodgeproof ("we"), handles when you use Lodgeproof, visit the website or contact us, and your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

The short version

  • Your jobs never leave your computer. Lodgeproof reads documents, photos and the facts in them on your own PC. We never receive them, see them or store them.
  • We hold only what you send us yourself: your name, email address, your firm, what you write to us, and, if you pay, invoice details.
  • No tracking. The website sets no cookies, loads nothing from other sites and has no analytics. The program has no telemetry.

What the program does with data

Lodgeproof runs on your Windows computer. Everything it reads and makes stays in its data folder on that computer: job folders you choose, the facts it reads, its findings, reports, the profiles of people who use it, and the record of who confirmed what.

It connects to the internet only:

  • to check for updates (program and rule updates), when you ask it to or when automatic checks are on. The request says which version you have and nothing else about you or your jobs; it goes to GitHub, which hosts the downloads (GitHub's privacy statement).

We will update this policy before Lodgeproof connects for anything else (such as checking a licence online).

"Send feedback" saves a text file on your computer and opens your own email program with the message filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. Details about a job are added only if you tick the box, and even then they are rule names and results, never names, addresses or documents.

You are the controller of the personal data in your jobs (householders, installers and so on). Because we never receive it, we are not your processor for it.

Your rights

You can ask us to: give you a copy of your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to how we use it; or move it. Email lodgeproof@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.
