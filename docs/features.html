Features

Everything Lodgeproof does happens on your computer, and every finding shows where it came from.

Reads the whole job

  • PDFs, Word files, zips and photos, including scans, faxes and iPhone pictures.
  • Files each document by what it is: assessment, design, handover pack, certificates.
  • Reads every page. Where a scan is hard to read, it shows you the page and asks you to confirm.

Checks against PAS 2035:2023

  • Every document the job needs, signed where it must be.
  • Dates in the right order: assessment, design, installation, handover, sign-off and lodgement.
  • The right people in each role, with qualifications and registrations in date.
  • The same address, postcode and property reference on every document.
  • Measures designed, installed and claimed all match.
  • Photos taken at the property, at the right stage, and not used on another job.
  • Rules for Wales and Scotland where they differ.

Each rule names its public source. In the beta, every finding is advisory.

Reports you can stand behind

  • A PDF or CSV report with your firm's name and logo.
  • Findings sorted into Must fix, Worth checking and Good practice, each with why it matters and how to fix it.
  • A clean report never says a job is compliant: it says how many checks ran and that none found an issue.

Remembers across jobs

  • The people on every job, and whose registration runs out soon.
  • Issues that keep coming back, by rule and by person.
  • Layouts of documents you have confirmed, so the next one is read without asking.

Built for an office

  • A profile for each person on a shared computer; what they confirm and export is recorded.
  • Signed rule updates, shown to you before they are installed.
  • Works offline. Uses under 300 MB of memory. No administrator needed to install.
