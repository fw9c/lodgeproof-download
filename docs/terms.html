Beta agreement and disclaimer

By entering a beta key in Lodgeproof you agree to the following.

What the beta is

  • Lodgeproof is beta software: unfinished, and still being tested. It may contain mistakes, miss problems, or report problems that are not there.
  • Findings are advisory. Its rules are written from public sources and have not yet been verified against the published standards by a qualified person. They are not a substitute for reading PAS 2035, PAS 2030 and your scheme's own requirements, or for your professional judgement.
  • Lodgeproof never says a job is compliant. A clean report means only that the checks it ran found no issue.
  • You remain responsible for every job you lodge, every certificate and every decision.

Your key

  • A beta key lets one person check up to 10 jobs over 30 days on one computer. It is for you; please do not share it.
  • The beta is free. We may end it, or change what it includes, at any time; we will email you first if we can. When it ends you can still open and export every job you checked.

Your data

  • Your documents stay on your computer. We never receive them (see the privacy policy).
  • Please do not send us customers' documents. If you send feedback about a job, describe it or send Lodgeproof's report.

Feedback

  • We would like your feedback, and may use it to improve Lodgeproof without paying you or naming you. We will not quote you publicly without asking.

Liability

  • The beta is provided "as is", without any promise that it is fit for a particular purpose, accurate or free of errors, to the extent the law allows.
  • We are not liable for any loss arising from your use of the beta, including a failed audit, a rejected lodgement, lost funding, or remedial work, to the extent the law allows. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law (such as for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or fraud).

General

  • Questions: lodgeproof@gmail.com.
