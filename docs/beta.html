The beta

Try Lodgeproof on your own jobs, free: 10 job checks over 30 days.

  1. Download it. The Windows installer (the file ending in setup.exe). The example job works straight away, without a key.
  2. Ask for a key. Email lodgeproof@gmail.com with your name and firm. We reply with a key, usually within a day.
  3. Check your jobs. Paste the key on the Jobs page and add a job folder.

What to expect

  • The beta is not code-signed yet, so Windows may warn that it protected your PC. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  • Findings are advisory. The rules are written from public sources and are not yet verified against the published standards by a qualified person.
  • When the beta ends you can still open every job you checked and save its report.
  • Beta firms are offered founding-customer terms: 50% off the first year. See plans.

Using a beta key means you agree to the beta terms.
