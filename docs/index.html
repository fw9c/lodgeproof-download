Make every job lodge-proof

Lodgeproof reads a retrofit job's paperwork and finds what would stop it passing audit, before the job is lodged. You review what it found and decide what to do.

It runs on your own computer, with or without an internet connection. Your customers' documents never leave it.

What it checks

  • Every document is there

    The assessment, design, ventilation assessment, handover pack and the rest, against PAS 2035:2023.

  • Dates in the right order

    A ventilation assessment dated after the install, a design signed off late, an evaluation booked too soon.

  • The right people

    Qualifications and registrations on record, in date, and no one doing two roles they should not.

  • Photos that prove the work

    Taken at the property, at the right stage, and not used on another job.

The professional decides

Lodgeproof does the legwork: it files the documents, reads every page, runs the checks and writes the report. Anything it is not sure of, it asks you to confirm. Nothing is decided for you, and every finding shows the exact page it was read from.
