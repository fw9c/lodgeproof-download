How it works

Three steps from a folder of paperwork to a report you can stand behind.

  1. Drop in the job. A folder, a zip, or a handful of PDFs, Word files and photos, including scans and iPhone pictures. Lodgeproof files each document and reads every page.
  2. Read what it found. Issues come in three kinds: Must fix, Worth checking and Good practice. Each one says why it matters, how to fix it, and shows the page and the words it was read from.
  3. Confirm and export. Where a scan is hard to read, you confirm the value. Then save a PDF or CSV report with your firm's name and logo, ready for your records.

What stays with you

  • Every document stays on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
  • It works without an internet connection.
  • Each person on a shared computer has their own profile, and what they confirm and export is recorded with their name.
  • Rule updates arrive as signed files, are shown to you before they are installed, and say which jobs to check again.

What it does not do

Lodgeproof never says a job is compliant. A clean report says how many checks were run and that none found an issue. Some things can only be checked on site, and the report lists them for you.
